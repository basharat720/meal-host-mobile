FoodPal connects you with home chefs in your area. Browse real home kitchens, order the dishes they cook, and follow your order from the kitchen to your door.

FIND HOME KITCHENS NEAR YOU
Browse kitchens or individual dishes, search by name, and filter to find what you're craving. Each kitchen has its own menu, opening hours, ratings and reviews from other customers. No sign-in is needed to look around.

ORDER IN A FEW TAPS
Add dishes to your cart, choose delivery or pickup, and place your order. Delivery is available in selected areas; everywhere else you can collect your order from the kitchen. Pay in cash when you pick up, or at the door when your order is delivered.

TRACK EVERY ORDER
See each order move from confirmed to ready to delivered, with notifications at every step. Active and past orders are kept in one place.

CHAT WITH THE CHEF
Once your order is confirmed, message the chef directly about anything: timings, directions or a special request.

CAN'T FIND IT? ASK FOR IT
Post a request for a dish nobody lists, and home chefs can offer to cook it for you.

SAVE YOUR FAVORITES
Tap the heart on any kitchen or dish to find it again later.

RATE AND REVIEW
After your meal, leave a rating and review to help other customers and the chefs themselves.

COOK FOR YOUR NEIGHBOURHOOD
Love to cook? Apply as a FoodPal chef. Once approved, you can publish your menu, set your opening hours, accept and prepare orders, bid on customer requests, and see your earnings, all from the same app.

Your account, your choice: you can delete your FoodPal account at any time from Profile, or at foodpal.co/delete-account.
