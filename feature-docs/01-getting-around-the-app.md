# Getting Around the App

## Two separate areas

The app has two completely separate areas with their own tab bars, and you are
only ever in one of them:

- **The customer area** — browsing kitchens, ordering, tracking orders.
- **The chef area** — the chef's own portal for their menu, orders and hours.

Which one you land in depends on your account. A chef opens the app in the
chef area; everyone else opens in the customer area. Someone who is both can
switch between them (see below).

You do **not** have to be signed in to browse. A brand-new visitor lands in
the customer area and can look through every kitchen and dish. Signing in is
only asked for at the point it is genuinely needed — adding to a cart,
checking out, posting a request.

## The customer tabs

| Tab | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Find Chefs — the list of kitchens, with search and filters. This is the tab the app opens on. |
| **Dishes** | The dish feed, browsing individual dishes rather than kitchens |
| **Orders** | Your orders, split into Active and Past |
| **Profile** | Your details, addresses, legal pages, sign out |

The cart, checkout, order confirmation, post-a-request and my-requests screens
exist too, but have no tab of their own — you reach them from wherever they
make sense, and a cart button floats above the tabs whenever you have
something in it.

**Home is Find Chefs, not the dish feed.** The kitchen is the thing a customer
is really choosing, so it leads. The website does the same, and its own
navigation labels that page "Home" too.

## The chef tabs

| Tab | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Today's orders and earnings, your rating, and the way into Earnings & Orders |
| **My Menu** | The dishes you publish, and adding or editing them |
| **Orders** | Incoming orders to accept, prepare and complete |
| **Requests** | Customer requests you can bid on |
| **Profile** | Your kitchen profile and account |

Availability (weekly opening hours) and Earnings & Orders have no tab — both
are reached from the Dashboard.

## Being both a chef and a customer

If your account holds both roles, a **Mode** section appears in your profile
with two choices: *Chef Mode* and *Customer Mode*. Picking one takes you
straight to that area and the app remembers it, so closing and reopening the
app puts you back where you were.

Switching is a real switch, not a shortcut: in Customer Mode you get the
customer experience throughout — the floating cart, the customer profile
fields, customer-coloured branding. In Chef Mode you get the kitchen.

Accounts with only one role never see the Mode section, because there is no
choice to offer.

> **Note:** you cannot yet *add* a role to an existing account from the app.
> Switching only works between roles the account already has. See
> [Where the App Differs from the Website](05-parity-with-the-website.md).
