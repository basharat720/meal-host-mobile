# Notifications & Opening a Link

## The bell

Every screen has a bell with a count of unread notifications. Tapping it opens
the notification centre, where each item can be marked read, or all of them at
once. The app checks for new ones every 30 seconds while open.

## Push notifications

The app asks permission to send notifications, but not the moment you open it
for the first time. It explains first, in its own words, why notifications are
useful — and only then triggers the system prompt. Declining that gentle ask
means the system prompt is never shown, so the one chance iOS gives an app to
ask is not wasted.

Chefs get notified about new orders and customer requests they can bid on;
customers about order progress and offers on their requests.

## The number on the app icon

The unread count is mirrored onto the app's icon badge, and clears as
notifications are read.

## Tapping a notification

Tapping a notification takes you to what it is about rather than just opening
the app:

| The notification is about | Where you land |
| --- | --- |
| An order | Your Orders, with that order opened and scrolled to |
| A request | That request's page |
| Chef business | The chef dashboard |
| Anything else | The notification centre |

This works whether the app was open, in the background, or fully closed when
the notification arrived.

For orders, if the notification names a specific order, the app switches to
the right tab (Active or Past, depending on the order's stage), expands that
order, and scrolls it into view. If it does not name one, you get the list.

## Links from emails

Order emails link to a specific order. **Those links currently open the
website, not the app** — even on a phone with the app installed.

Making them open the app requires PakwanHus to publish two small verification
files on pakwanhus.com, one for Apple and one for Android, proving that the
website and the app belong together. Until that is done, the app can only be
opened by its own `mealhost://` links, not by ordinary web addresses.
