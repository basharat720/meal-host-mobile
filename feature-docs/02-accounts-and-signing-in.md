# Accounts & Signing In

## Two ways in

Customers and chefs sign up on separate screens, because they are asked for
different things. Both use an email address and a password.

## What customer signup asks for

One screen: full name, email, password, an optional phone number, and an
address. Then the Terms and Privacy Policy must be ticked.

## What chef signup asks for

Two steps.

**Step 1 — who you are:** first and last name *as they appear on your CNIC*
(the two are kept separate because the name has to match an identity
document), email, password, and a phone number, which is required for chefs.

**Step 2 — your kitchen:** kitchen name, a description of at least 20
characters, your specialities, a profile photo, and your kitchen address.
Then the **Partner Agreement** must be ticked, alongside the Terms and Privacy
Policy.

**Kitchen name is what customers see.** Not your own name — it is the name on
your listing, your menu and your dashboard. If it is ever left blank, your own
name is used instead.

Ticking the Partner Agreement is recorded against your account with the date
and time, because it is a commercial agreement with Bridging People
(SMC-Private) Limited. The full text is a screen of its own, linked from the
tickbox.

New chef accounts start as **pending** and cannot publish a menu or take
orders until an admin approves them. Until then the dashboard shows what is
still outstanding.

## Phone numbers

Every phone field in the app is the same: a country flag and dialling code on
the left, the rest of the number beside it. Pakistan is pre-selected. The
number is checked against the rules of whichever country is chosen — a
Norwegian mobile is 8 digits, a Pakistani one is 10 — rather than one generic
digit count, and is always stored in the same international format.

## Addresses

Address fields search real places as you type and offer suggestions, and there
is a **Locate me** button that reads your position and fills in the address. In
both cases the exact coordinates are captured along with the text, which is
what lets the app work out distances and, later, whether you are inside the
delivery area.

Suggestions are biased towards Lahore, since that is where PakwanHus operates.

## Verifying your email

After signing up you are asked to confirm your email address. The app sends a
PakwanHus-branded email, checks every few seconds whether you have clicked the
link, and moves you on by itself once you have.

There is a **Resend** button with a 60-second countdown. The countdown starts
already running, because signing up has just sent one.

## Why there are no social sign-in buttons

There are none at the moment. There was a "Continue with Google" button on all
four screens, but it never worked in any version of the app — it always failed
with an error — so it has been hidden rather than left as a dead end. Email
and password work everywhere.

Getting Google sign-in working needs Google credentials and a new build of the
app; it cannot be switched on from the app's code alone. Facebook sign-in,
which the website has, has not been built for the app yet.

## Forgotten passwords

Enter your email and a reset link is sent. The app will not tell you whether
an account exists for that address — it says "if an account exists for that
email, we've sent a reset link" either way — so the screen cannot be used to
find out who has an account.
