# Becoming FoodPal

The app is called **FoodPal**. It was called PakwanHus. This document covers
the rename itself — the name, the icon, the splash screen and the logo — which
[The FoodPal Look in the App](08-the-foodpal-look-in-the-app.md) deliberately
left alone when it changed the colours.

> **The short version.** Same app, same account, same orders, new name and new
> face. Nobody has to reinstall anything and nothing anyone saved is lost.

## What people will notice

- **The home-screen icon** becomes Pal the Ghost — the chef-hatted mascot — in
  white on brand blue.
- **The name under the icon** becomes FoodPal.
- **The opening screen** shows Pal on a blue field instead of the old green
  house mark.
- **The logo in the app's header** becomes the real FoodPal lockup: Pal in a
  rounded blue tile, next to the word FoodPal. It is the artwork the designer
  supplied and the same file the website uses, not a lookalike rebuilt from
  fonts and shapes.
- **The word "PakwanHus" is gone** from every screen, including the terms,
  privacy, refund and chef agreement pages, and from both English and
  Norwegian.

## The app does not become a different app

This is the part worth being explicit about, because it is the part that would
be expensive to get wrong.

The app keeps its identity on the App Store and Google Play. It is the same
listing, so **every existing install updates in place**, and the ratings and
reviews the app has built up stay attached to it. Nobody is asked to find and
download a new app, and no one is left stranded on an old one.

The same goes for what is stored on the phone. **A cart that was filled before
the update is still there afterwards**, along with a saved delivery location
and a signed-in session.

## Links that already exist keep working

Links that open the app — the ones in old emails, push notifications and
messages people have already received — keep working. The app now answers to
both its new name and its old one, so a link written before the rename opens
exactly as it did.

## The header logo no longer changes per person

The logo used to show different artwork depending on who was signed in: a
chef's hat for chefs, crossed utensils for customers, and the app mark for
everyone else. FoodPal has **one mark**, so everyone now sees the same logo.
Chefs and customers are told apart by what the screens say and do, not by a
different badge in the corner.

## What is deliberately not changing yet

- **The website address and the support email.** Mail still goes to the same
  place it always did, and the site is still at the same address. Changing a
  domain affects email delivery and search results and is being handled
  separately, not smuggled in with a rename. The website has made the same
  choice, so the two stay consistent.
- **The app's listing text and screenshots** on both stores. Those are written
  and uploaded outside the app.

## Where the artwork comes from

Every piece of FoodPal artwork in the app is copied from the website's set
rather than redrawn, so the two cannot drift apart. The rider app already
works this way. If the designer issues new artwork, it is re-copied, never
re-traced.
